Privacy Policy — Block Drop Rush

Effective date: 10 October 2026

Developer: PolicyReach Technologies

Contact: policyreach@gmail.com

This policy describes how the Android game Block Drop Rush ("the app") handles information.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or device data.

What the app does not do

  • It does not request the Android INTERNET permission and cannot send data over the network.
  • It has no accounts, sign-in, analytics, crash reporting, advertising or in-app purchases active.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Data stored on your device only

To work, the app saves a small amount of game data locally on your device:

  • best scores and local top-10 score lists;
  • settings (sound, haptics, reduced motion, tutorial status);
  • game progress (saved Classic game, current level, coins, daily missions, achievements, unlocked themes, daily reward and challenge history).

This data never leaves the app's own storage, except that if you have Android device backup turned on, Android may include it in your personal Google backup (governed by Google's privacy policy). You can delete all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Future changes

A future version may add optional features such as rewarded ads, which would involve third-party services and data collection. If that happens, this policy will be updated before that version is released, the "Effective date" above will change, and any consent required by law will be requested in the app.
