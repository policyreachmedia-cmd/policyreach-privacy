Privacy Policy — Block Drop Rush

Effective date: 10 October 2026

Developer: PolicyReach Technologies

Contact: support@bondreply.com

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game Block Drop Rush ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. It applies to all versions of the app published by PolicyReach Technologies.

1. Summary

  • You can play without an account. We (PolicyReach Technologies) do not collect, store on our own servers, sell or rent your personal information.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The app may offer optional rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob. You only see one if you choose to watch it, for example to get an in-game bonus. When ads are used, Google may collect certain data as described in Section 4.
  • Versions of the app with ads switched off do not request internet access and send no data anywhere.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves game data locally in its private storage on your device:

  • best scores and local top-10 score lists;
  • settings (sound, haptics, reduced motion, tutorial status);
  • game progress (saved games, current level, coins, daily missions, achievements, unlocked themes, daily reward and challenge history).

This data is not sent to us. If Android device backup is enabled, Android may include it in your personal Google backup, which is governed by Google's privacy policy. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, files, microphone or camera data. The app has no sign-in or user accounts, and we do not sell your data.

4. Advertising — Google AdMob (optional rewarded ads)

The app may display optional rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. Ads are never required to play. They appear only when you tap a button offering a reward (for example, extra coins or a refreshed set of pieces), and a reward is given only after the ad is completed.

When an ad is requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process:

  • Device identifiers: the Android Advertising ID (or an app-set ID where the Advertising ID is unavailable);
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, app version, and similar technical data;
  • Approximate (coarse) location: derived from your IP address (not GPS);
  • Ad interaction data: ads requested, viewed and clicked, and whether a rewarded ad was completed;
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance information related to the ads SDK.

Google uses this data to:

  • deliver and measure ads (including personalised ads where you have consented);
  • run analytics;
  • detect and prevent fraud and abuse;
  • keep its services secure.

Data is encrypted in transit. Please read how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland) and personalised ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before ads are personalised, as required by applicable law (GDPR, UK GDPR) and Google's EU User Consent Policy. You can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings ("Privacy options"), when available. Where required, we also show US state privacy messages through the same platform.

If you do not consent, Google may show non-personalised ads. These are based on context (such as the app itself) and coarse location rather than your past behaviour. Non-personalised ads may still use identifiers for frequency capping, aggregated reporting, and fraud and abuse prevention.

You can also control ads on your device at any time:

  • Opt out of ads personalisation: Android Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation (wording varies by device).
  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads) → Reset advertising ID / Delete advertising ID.

6. Children's privacy

Block Drop Rush is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad requests are configured to comply with Google's policies. If ads are served to users who may be children, we use Google's child-directed and under-age-of-consent settings, which disable personalised advertising for them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data retention

  • Game data on your device stays there until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Data processed by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's retention policies (https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention).
  • We do not keep copies of any of this data.

8. Security

Local game data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access under Android's security model. Communication between the ads SDK and Google uses encryption (HTTPS/TLS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use reasonable measures appropriate to a game that holds no personal data on our side.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent, and to data portability. Because we do not hold your data on our own servers, you can usually exercise these rights by:

  • deleting the app's local data (clear storage or uninstall);
  • changing your ad consent in the app's Privacy options, or your device's ads settings;
  • contacting Google about data processed by AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

For any request or question, including deletion requests, email support@bondreply.com. We will respond within 30 days. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Third-party links

Ads may link to third-party websites or apps that have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their content or practices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when features or third-party services change. The updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will be announced in the app or its store listing before they take effect, and where the law requires it we will ask for your consent again.
