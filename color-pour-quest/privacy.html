Color Pour Quest – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026
Developer: PolicyReach Technologies (Nigeria)
Contact: support@bondreply.com

Summary

Color Pour Quest is an offline puzzle game. We do not ask for your name, email, location or any account. Game progress (level, coins, settings, cosmetics) is stored only on your device. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects certain device data as described below.

Data stored on your device

Progress, in-game coins, achievements, daily-reward streak and settings are saved locally using Android DataStore. They may be included in your device's Android backup (Google account) if you have backup enabled. We have no server and cannot see this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob) and consent

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). These may collect and share with Google: device and advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID, app set ID), IP address (used to estimate approximate location), app interactions with ads, diagnostics and crash/performance data. This data is used for showing ads, ad measurement, frequency capping and fraud prevention, and (where you consent) personalised ads. Data is encrypted in transit.

If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland (or another region where required), you will be asked for consent through Google's consent message. You can change your choice any time via Settings › Ad privacy choices in the game. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

In-game coins

Coins are virtual, in-game only, cannot be bought, sold, transferred, withdrawn or exchanged for money or prizes, and have no cash value.

Children

The game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on your location (e.g. Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, GDPR) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we hold no personal data on servers, most requests are handled by uninstalling the app, resetting your Advertising ID, or contacting Google about ad data. Email support@bondreply.com with any request.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change; the effective date above will change accordingly.
